    BOTens Management Visits Burapha University and EEC Automation Park in Thailand

    On 12 November 2026, BOTens’ Chief Operating Officer (COO) and Chief Technology Officer (CTO) visited Burapha University (BUU) in Thailand, including the EEC Automation Park, located within the university’s Faculty of Engineering.

    During the visit, BOTens management engaged in constructive discussions with representatives from Burapha University and EEC Automation Park, gaining valuable insights into Thailand’s ongoing developments in robotics, industrial automation, and smart manufacturing. The visit also provided an opportunity to exchange perspectives on the evolving technology landscape and explore potential areas of collaboration.

    About Burapha University (BUU)

    Burapha University, located in Chonburi Province, is a leading public university in eastern Thailand, offering a broad range of academic programmes across disciplines such as engineering, science, medicine, and social sciences.

    The university is committed to advancing higher education, academic research, technological innovation, and professional talent development. It also plays an important role in supporting regional education, research, and industrial development.

    About EEC Automation Park

    Located within the Faculty of Engineering at Burapha University, EEC Automation Park was established through a joint initiative between the university and Mitsubishi Electric Factory Automation (Thailand). It serves as a technology learning, demonstration, and workforce development centre supporting the industrial advancement of Thailand’s Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC).

    The centre focuses on Industry 4.0 technologies, including industrial automation, robotics, smart manufacturing, automated production systems, and digital manufacturing.

    Through technology demonstrations, hands-on training, and professional skills development, EEC Automation Park helps strengthen industrial capabilities and accelerate the adoption of advanced automation technologies in Thailand.

    Appreciation to Burapha University and EEC Automation Park

    BOTens would like to extend its sincere appreciation to Burapha University and the EEC Automation Park team for their warm hospitality, informative presentations, and valuable time throughout the visit.

    The visit provided BOTens with valuable insights into Thailand’s growing robotics, industrial automation, and smart manufacturing sectors, while offering an opportunity to exchange knowledge and perspectives with representatives from the university and the centre.

    BOTens looks forward to maintaining positive engagement and exploring potential opportunities for future collaboration.

    Ecosystem NewsNovember 2026

    Mongbotics Advances to Protocol Camp Cohort 10 Mid-Demo Day

    Mongbotics, an ecosystem partner of BOTens, has advanced to the mid-demonstration stage of the Protocol Camp Cohort 10 startup competition. Mongbotics is the only Thai team to qualify for this cohort. The team will present its full-stack robotics solution at the Mid-Demo Day in Bangkok on November 28. Mongbotics will also bring a physical robot to Binance Blockchain Week for a public demonstration of its end-to-end technology, with BOTens providing support as an ecosystem partner.

    The Protocol Camp Cohort 10 competition officially launched on September 14, 2026. Following multiple rounds of highly selective screening and rigorous interviews, only 10 startup teams were selected. With a comprehensive technology roadmap and a clearly defined industry value proposition, Mongbotics stood out among the competing projects, making it the only team from the host country, Thailand, to advance.

    The competition requires formal weekly reviews throughout the program. Selected teams must continuously demonstrate project progress, technology deployment capabilities, and innovation potential to a judging panel of industry mentors and technology experts. Mongbotics has steadily refined its project through successive assessments, completing key milestones including its debut at the Cohort 10 kickoff and the presentation of its project vision at Korea Blockchain Week. The team is now preparing a live functional demonstration and a refined product presentation for the Mid-Demo Showcase.

    Competing in the AI-Enhanced/Vibe Coding track, Mongbotics integrates four core modules: the MongBot autonomous delivery robot, the MongCore control and management system, the MongChain on-chain identity and ownership framework, and the MongMarket peer-to-peer marketplace. The project has earned recognition from the judging panel for its exploration of practical applications in autonomous robotics. With autonomous robotics technology still at an emerging stage in Thailand, the project has the potential to promote AI adoption in the country and support science, technology, and innovation development in the Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC). It offers a practical example supporting Thailand’s goal of becoming a leading technology hub in Southeast Asia.

    The team is currently preparing for the next stages of the competition. It will also continue small-scale explorations with BOTens in areas including robot IP operations and on-chain identity and ownership verification, jointly building a broader base of reusable practical experience for the local deployment of AI-powered robots.

    OUR ROBOTS

    BOTens D1

    A six-wheel indoor-outdoor integrated delivery robot with high payload, long endurance and strong mobility

    Explore BOTens D1Contact Us

    Key Features · Key Technologies

    Hardware Layer

    Intelligent robot chassis, D1 high-payload platform, lightweight platform and multi-scenario upper-structure solutions. Modular design, customizable by scenario.

    Autonomous Driving Layer

    Integrated indoor-outdoor autonomous driving, supporting continuous localization, perception, planning and control in unstructured indoor and outdoor environments.

    Access Layer

    AIoT access-control and elevator integration, low-cost elevator control, access-control AIoT and flexible door openers, solving the robot access bottlenecks of “passing through doors, gates and elevators”.

    Operations Layer

    Dispatch platform + localized operations, including route planning and dispatch, status monitoring, exception handling and data closed-loop management, supported by local delivery and continuous operating capabilities.

    Core Capabilities

    High Payload

    Recommended payload ≤50 kg, maximum static payload 200 kg, supporting large-item and multi-stop one-to-many delivery.

    Long Endurance

    LiFePO4 battery, 12-15 hours of endurance, supports both charging and battery swapping, with a full charge in approximately 3 hours.

    Strong Mobility

    Six-wheel chassis, gradeability ≤30°, obstacle clearance <3 cm, supporting continuous indoor-outdoor operation.

    High Intelligence

    Equipped with integrated indoor-outdoor autonomous driving, enabling autonomous localization and navigation, dynamic obstacle avoidance and route planning.

    Full Perception

    Multi-sensor fusion of 3D LiDAR, wide-angle waterproof cameras, 3D depth cameras and ultrasonic radar to accurately identify pedestrians, vehicles and obstacles.

    Customizable

    Body height and upper structure can be customized by scenario, using a modular platform to quickly adapt to different industries.

    Unmanned Operation

    Together with the dispatch platform and AIoT access infrastructure, the robot can support 24-hour unmanned operation and remote O&M.

    Detail Display

    Hemispherical Non-Repetitive 3D LiDAR

    Creates a 360° 3D map of the surrounding environment - detecting obstacles, people and walls from all directions.

    Infrared High-Dynamic Wide-Angle Waterproof Camera

    Maintains clear vision in low light, rain or strong light - helping the robot recognize objects and read signs.

    3D Depth Camera

    Measures the distance to nearby objects - critical for collision avoidance and clearance assessment.

    Anti-Collision Strip / Bumper

    Protects the robot and cushions impact if it accidentally contacts an obstacle.

    Ultrasonic Radar

    Detects objects at very close range (for example, when someone walks in front) - providing additional safety in narrow spaces.
